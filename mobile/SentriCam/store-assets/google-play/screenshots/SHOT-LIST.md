# SentriCam Google Play screenshot plan

Use real application screens only. Recommended export: **1080 × 1920 PNG/JPEG**.

1. **راقب البث المباشر** — Live camera view with a connected device.
2. **تحكم في التسجيل عن بُعد** — Recording and camera-control actions.
3. **اكتشاف حركة قابل للضبط** — Motion toggle, sensitivity and duration settings.
4. **راجع تسجيلاتك بسهولة** — Recording library, filters and playback.
5. **تابع حالة اتصال أجهزتك** — Healthy / recovering / offline connection states.
6. **اعمل داخل شبكتك المحلية** — Pairing or Local Hub setup screen.

Before capture:
- Use non-sensitive demo device names and test footage.
- Hide local IP addresses, pairing secrets and personal notifications.
- Capture the same app version that will be uploaded as the AAB.
- Do not advertise cloud or AI features unless they are enabled in that release.
