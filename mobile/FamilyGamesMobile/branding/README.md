# Family Games branding assets

- `family-games-lamma-master.png` is the unchanged approved Family Games / Lamma artwork. Keep it as the high-resolution source for future splash, about, and iOS branding work.
- `family-games-launcher-master.png` is the launcher-optimized derivative. It intentionally removes the small wordmark and keeps the family and controller prominent inside common adaptive-icon masks.

The launcher derivative is a product branding asset, not shared business logic. Platform packaging may resize it, but must not mirror, stretch, or add app-specific text.
